## Statement

Anyone who works on the project, person or agent, does so inside a Process Instance of a kind that reaches Loop v2.0.0.

## Rationale

The loop structure gives every piece of work a Reservoir and a retrospective, so frictions and findings are not lost. A process that inherits Loop v2.0.0 is specialised for the development lifecycle (see the Decision on Roadmap, Milestone and Issue loops).
